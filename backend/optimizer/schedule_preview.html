Notes from this round

Reverse-engineered from a real, human-tuned week. Gym, breakfast, lunch, and dinner now sit on the calendar as real fixed blocks (not tasks) — the same mechanism as a locked class. Friday and Saturday night are hard-protected (no work 7pm–midnight), Sunday gets its own much lower load cap instead of the same number as every other day, and a new preference rewards studying a course shortly after that course's own lecture — the "notes right after class" pattern real students actually use.

Titles got a pass too. Raw source names ("Recitation Quiz 3") are now instructions ("Study for Recitation 3"), and multi-part tasks share one repeated title instead of a "(part i/N)" suffix — position on the calendar already says it's ongoing.

Compute time: quality is already close to final at 1 second (99.99%+ optimized either way); the 10-second budget buys almost nothing further. CP-SAT never quite proves strict optimality on a model this size, but the gap stays under 0.01% the whole time — a non-issue in practice.

Still-open gap: Midterm 1 / Exam 1 are still movable blocks with a deadline, not a fixed time like a lecture — a schema question (see backend/optimizer/README.md), not a preference.
