Notes from this round

No preferences = one brutal day. With only "place everything, prefer earlier," CP-SAT crammed 900 of the day's 900 available minutes (8am–11pm, zero gaps) into Saturday alone, then spilled into Sunday, then stopped — nothing touches the following ten days. Four preferences (a soft 4-hour/day cap, an evening-hours reward, a hard 15-minute buffer between sessions, and a penalty for landing two sessions of the same task on the same day) turn that into a normal-looking week: busiest day drops to 4.5 hours, 90% of work lands in the evening, and zero sessions butt up against each other.

A real gap this surfaced: Midterm 1 and Exam 1 are shown as movable blocks the optimizer can place anywhere before a deadline — but a real exam happens at one specific, non-negotiable time, like a lecture. Right now nothing in the data distinguishes "finish this before X" from "this literally happens at X." That needs a schema answer (probably: exams materialize as locked blocks, same as `courses.meeting_times`), not a scheduling preference.
